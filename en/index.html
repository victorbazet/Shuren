Your frequent questions

Everything people ask me before getting started.

What happens if I stop the subscription?

You can stop at any time. The site goes offline at the end of the current month. Your content and texts belong to you — I transfer them on simple request.

How many changes can I request per month?

As many as needed to keep your business alive: change a photo, add a service, update your hours, publish news. Anything that is routine content is included. For big projects (full redesign, an entirely new page), we discuss it together.

Are there hidden fees or a commitment?

None. It's €299/month, period. No creation fee, no extra hosting to pay, no 12-month contract. You start when you want, you stop when you want.

What if I don't know how to use a computer at all?

That's exactly why this plan exists. You have nothing to learn, nothing to install, nothing to configure. You send me your texts and photos by email or WhatsApp, I take care of the rest.

How long until my site is online?

Count about 2 weeks between our first conversation and going live. One week to build the site from your brief, one week for adjustments and launch on your domain name.

What is an AI agent, concretely?

It's an assistant built into your site that answers your visitors' questions in real time — like a virtual employee available 24/7. It's trained on your information (hours, pricing, services) and always replies consistently with your brand.

Can my AI agent reply to my customers at night and on weekends?

That's exactly its role. Your agent doesn't sleep, doesn't take weekends off and replies within seconds at any hour. No more messages left unanswered for 48h — your customers get an immediate reply, and you pick up the urgent requests first thing the next morning.
