FAQ

Your frequent questions.

Everything people ask me before getting started.

How much does an AI agent cost for my business?

From €50 per month. The price depends on the agents you pick and how many venues you have: we set it together during a free 15-minute call, and it doesn't change afterwards. No commitment.

What happens after the 2-month pilot?

You decide: move to the Essential or Operational plan, or stop, with no fees and no notice period. During the pilot we measure together what the agent saves you, so you decide based on real results.

What happens if I stop the subscription?

You can stop at any time. Your agent is switched off at the end of the current month, and your data as well as your configurations (knowledge base, history, access) are handed over on request. Nothing is held hostage.

How many updates can I request per month?

As many as needed: new price, new service, changed hours or procedures — I update your agent's knowledge with no limit and no extra charge. For a new agent or a new integration, we discuss it together.

Are there hidden fees or a commitment?

None. Your monthly fee is agreed together before we start, based on your business and number of venues, and then it doesn't change. No setup fee, no extra infrastructure to pay for, no 12-month contract. You start when you want, you stop when you want.

What if I'm not comfortable with computers at all?

That's exactly why I handle everything. There's nothing to learn, nothing to install, nothing to configure. You send me your information by email or WhatsApp, and I take care of everything else.

How long before my AI agent is up and running?

Count on 1 to 2 weeks between our first conversation and going live: a few days to design the agent and train it on your business, a few days for testing and fine-tuning with you.

What exactly is an AI agent?

A virtual employee available around the clock: it answers your customers, qualifies your prospects, generates your schedules or your reports — plugged into the channels you already use (WhatsApp, Google Business, email, your website). It's trained on your information and always answers in a way that's consistent with your brand.

Can my AI agent answer customers at night and on weekends?

That's exactly its job. Your agent doesn't sleep, doesn't take weekends off and replies within seconds at any hour. No more messages left unanswered for 48 hours — your customers get an immediate reply, and you pick up the urgent requests first thing the next morning.
