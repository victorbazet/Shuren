EO Family: a restaurant group,
and not a single review left unanswered.

Act I — The situation

Four restaurants in peak season, run by hand.

Four venues in the middle of the season, hundreds of Google reviews a year, schedules redone every week on the corner of a table, stock tracked from memory. At that scale, an ignored negative review or an undetected shrinkage point aren't details: they're lines on the P&L.

Act II — The rollout

Four agents, plugged into what was already there.

No software to learn. The Shuren agents connected to the tools already in place: Google review replies in each venue's own tone, a consolidated activity report every morning, schedules generated from contracts and the booking outlook, stock alerts and automatic supplier follow-ups.

  • Daily report agent

Act III — The outcome

The margin defends itself.

≈ 40 h of admin work given back to the teams every month
